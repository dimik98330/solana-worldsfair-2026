01 / COUPON

Transfers do not rewrite old rights.

Investor 01 records ten bonds and a 500-unit coupon. After transferring two bonds, the current position is eight; the coupon remains 500.

02 / REDEMPTION

Pay principal. Burn the bonds.

At maturity, principal follows the current position. Payment and token burn occur together; repeated claims are rejected by the program.

03 / VOTING

Snapshot weights. One ballot.

Recorded positions determine voting weight. The demonstrated Yes vote has weight ten. Votes are informational and cannot change cash terms.
