## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: an issuer/operator placing a permissioned bond and servicing its corporate actions. Secondary: registered holders inspecting rights, claiming payments and voting. Hackathon judges need a reproducible demonstration and explicit evidence scope.

## Product Purpose

Show who is entitled, the exact amount owed, the authorized next operation and its confirmed outcome. The user selected KASE's corporate-action challenge and explicitly requested a complete product, stronger backend and now a clearer, more professional desktop redesign.

## Operating Context

React19/Vite8/TypeScript7, Solana Kit8/Wallet Standard, Node22.14 HTTP relay, SQLite, Anchor/classic SPL Token. Issuer workflow: terms → register/place → full reserve → activation → record/payment → maturity/burn; holders sign claims and informational votes. API/contracts and on-chain terms are preserved by the redesign.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Maximum16 registered holders/8 coupons. Fixed record rights survive transfer/burn, principal pays and burns atomically, full prefunding, no issuer withdrawal. Current real verification is localnet; devnet program remains unfunded. Normal wallet signing is external; generated signer workspace is separately identified. No bank/KASE API, real assets, customer traction or regulatory certification is established.

## Product Principles

Use concise working labels, exact records, clear states and contextual details. Viewing a holder is independent of signing authority. Display timezone defaults to the browser, with wallet-independent preferences.

Latest registry-first rebuild responds to another direct visual rejection: show the payment object and its holders before reserve mechanics, group exact related figures, keep one issue/account toolbar, and distinguish missing connectivity from an empty workspace. Current composition and measured acceptance targets are in docs/frontend/REGISTRY-FIRST.md; prior screenshots and review passes are not owner acceptance.

Latest desktop feedback requires the work surface to use available width, with no fixed1360/900px content caps, a single text-field focus stroke, restrained status symbols, and original path-drawn BondTrace lettering. This supersedes earlier wordmark geometry and form-width decisions.
