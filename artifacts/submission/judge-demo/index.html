    Fund and activate

  3. 03

    Record and pay

    Capture the due record date. Historical coupon rights stay fixed when bonds later move.

  4. 04

    Retire and reconcile

    Pay principal and burn together. Inspect every claim, informational vote and retained receipt.

What is implemented

Financial lifecycle logic.

Solana terms, registry, record dates, exact entitlement, token payments, burn and voting. The backend reads one confirmed context, reconciles the numbers and retains signed operations before relay.

Prototype boundaries

Test assets, explicit scope.

Maximum 16 holders and 8 coupons, full prefunding, no issuer withdrawal. Bank/fiat rails, KASE API, custody and legal securities registration are not integrated. No partnership, real customers or production audit is claimed.
